# Locora

A hyperlocal platform that connects people with nearby businesses based on what they need and what's available around them.

## Overview

Finding something locally can be surprisingly inconvenient.

You might need a product urgently, want to find a nearby service, or simply want to know which shops around you currently have offers or new stock. At the same time, local businesses often struggle to reach customers who are actually nearby and looking for what they sell.

Locora aims to bridge that gap.

Users can discover nearby offers and businesses, or post a request describing something they need. Relevant businesses within the selected area can then respond to the request.

In simple terms:

> Businesses can say what they have.  
> Customers can say what they need.  
> Locora connects them.

## Core Features

### Nearby Offers

Businesses can publish:

- Discounts and promotions
- New inventory
- Restocked products
- Limited-time offers
- Seasonal campaigns
- New services

Users can browse these offers based on their location and selected search radius.

### "I'm Looking For..."

Instead of searching through multiple shops, users can simply describe what they need.

For example:

- "I need a birthday cake for tomorrow"
- "Looking for formal shoes under ₹1500"
- "Need someone to stitch a kurta"
- "Need a scientific calculator"
- "Looking for a laptop repair shop"

The request can include:

- Description
- Category
- Budget
- Quantity
- Required-by time
- Preferred distance
- Images
- Additional details

### Local Business Matching

Once a request is broadcast, nearby businesses that can fulfill it can respond.

Users can then compare businesses based on:

- Distance
- Price
- Rating
- Reviews
- Availability
- Response time

### Business Profiles

Each business has a lightweight profile containing:

- Business information
- Products and services
- Current offers
- Ratings and reviews
- Opening hours
- Location

### Location & Exposure Radius

Users can choose how far Locora should search for businesses and offers.

Available ranges include:

- 500 m
- 1 km
- 2 km
- 5 km
- 10 km

The MVP includes an interactive map showing the selected area and nearby businesses.
